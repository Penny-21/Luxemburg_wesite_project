Empowering Women in Tech

We want every participant to feel that AI is not just for tech experts; it's for everyone. By building digital literacy, encouraging critical thinking, and opening doors to inclusive networks, we aim to reduce the gender gap in AI usage and prevent future inequalities in the workplace.
Together, we will create a generation of TECH QUEENS who lead with innovations, empathy, and resilience.
