Amities Luxembourg-Armenie

Luxembourg Project Coordinator

Leading the charge in promoting intercultural dialogue and empowering diverse communities across Europe through innovative educational projects.

Our Mission

ALA's mission is to promote and improve Luxembourgish society while encouraging respect and understanding for cultural variety. Our vision is for a harmonious society where people from many cultural origins work together to address global challenges and promote sustainable development.

Key Objectives

Promote intercultural, inter-faith, and inter-community dialogue

Foster meaningful communication among diverse backgrounds

Use cultural strengths to address global challenges

Empower diverse communities for collaborative solutions

Promote visibility and respect for Luxembourg's cultures

Organize international youth educational projects

Partner

APS Senza Confini

La Spezia, Italy Strategic Partner

A dynamic Social Promotion Association inspiring and empowering young people to become active changemakers in their communities and beyond.

Our Mission

Senza Confini is a Social Promotion Association founded in 2020 with the mission to inspire and empower young people to take an active role in shaping their communities and society. We focus on fostering active citizenship among youth, addressing key challenges such as climate change, gender equality, democracy, and social inclusion.
